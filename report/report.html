Channel survey

* current channel★ lowest overlap-weighted score○ nearby AP seen on that channel

Counts other visible Wi-Fi networks per channel (above -80dBm) from this device's position at scan time — not traffic volume, not non-Wi-Fi interference, and 2.4GHz only: a 5GHz network, even from the same router, is invisible to this scan. The bars above are the raw, unweighted count per channel; the recommended channel (firmware Build 9b+) is picked using an adjacent-channel-overlap-weighted score instead — a 20MHz-wide channel's real airtime is affected by neighbors even when nothing sits exactly on it, so each candidate channel (1/6/11) sums every channel's count scaled by a triangular weight that's strongest at zero channel-separation and fades to nothing by 5 channels apart, the same-shaped kernel just centered differently per candidate — which is why the recommended channel can differ from whichever bar looks shortest. (An earlier firmware build, 9a, scored this with three differently-sized flat windows instead, which under- or over-counted channel 11 relative to channel 1 regardless of actual noise — logs from that build used the same CHANNEL_SURVEY format, so this panel reads them fine, just know the recommended value in an old 9a log may not match what 9b would have picked from the same raw counts.) If your AP is on Auto/ACS channel selection, its own controller sees far more (airtime utilization, sometimes spectrum-level interference, a fixed antenna position) and its channel choice should generally be trusted over this reading — treat this panel as a local snapshot, not a recommendation to override it. Needs firmware Build 7.1 or later; older logs won't have this panel. The small circles above a bar mark nearby APs seen on that channel at the latest nearby-AP scan (below) — that scan and this channel survey are logged independently and aren't guaranteed to be from the same moment, so treat the overlap as approximate.
