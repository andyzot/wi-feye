Channel survey

* current channel★ lowest overlap-weighted score○ nearby AP seen on that channel

Counts other visible Wi-Fi networks per channel (above -80dBm) from this device's position at scan time — not traffic volume, not non-Wi-Fi interference, and 2.4GHz only: a 5GHz network, even from the same router, is invisible to this scan. The bars above are the raw, unweighted count per channel; the recommended channel (firmware Build 9b+) is picked using an adjacent-channel-overlap-weighted score instead. If your AP is on Auto/ACS channel selection, its own controller sees far more than this reading does — treat this panel as a local snapshot, not a recommendation to override it. Needs firmware Build 7.1 or later. The small circles above a bar mark nearby APs seen on that channel at the latest nearby-AP scan (below) — that scan and this channel survey are logged independently and aren't guaranteed to be from the same moment, so treat the overlap as approximate.
